Common questions

How do I get an account?

AWT Hub accounts are issued by Awtoyoly to its employees. Your HR department or IT administrator creates the account and gives you your username and password. There is no public sign-up.

I forgot my password

Contact your IT administrator or write to the address above. For security, passwords are reset only after your identity is confirmed.

I don't receive notifications

Open the device settings and make sure notifications are allowed for AWT Hub. On iPhone also check that Focus / Do Not Disturb is not blocking the app. Then sign out and sign in again in the app.

Calls don't connect

Allow microphone and camera access for AWT Hub in the device settings and check your internet connection. If the problem continues, send us the time of the call and both participants' usernames.

How do I delete my account or my data?

Write to the address above from your work email or ask your administrator. Account and data deletion follows the privacy policy.
