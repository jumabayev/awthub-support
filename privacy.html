Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

AWT Hub (“the app”) is provided by Awtoyoly for its employees.

What we collect

  • Account data: your name, username, email address and the employee record your account is linked to.
  • Content you create: messages, voice messages, photos, videos, files, tasks, comments, notes, calendar events and reports.
  • Location: only when you choose to share your location in a chat.
  • Device data: a push notification token and a device identifier, used to deliver notifications to your device.

How we use it

Only to provide the app’s features: messaging, calls, tasks, calendar, notes, files and notifications. We do not sell your data, do not show ads and do not track you across other apps or websites.

Where it is stored

On Awtoyoly’s own servers. Push notifications are delivered through Apple Push Notification service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging; they receive only the data needed to deliver a notification.

Access and retention

Your data is available to you and, where your role requires it, to authorised colleagues (for example, members of the same chat or task). Data is kept while your company account is active and is handled under Awtoyoly’s internal policies afterwards.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data from your company administrator or at babamuratj@awtoyoly.com.
